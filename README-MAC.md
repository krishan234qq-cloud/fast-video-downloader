# Fast Video Downloader — macOS

**Made by [NioKrishan](https://www.instagram.com/nightlander_krishan/)**

Nothing to install beforehand. Everything is bundled inside the app.

---

## How to Install

1. Double-click `Fast Video Downloader-1.0.0.dmg`
2. Drag **Fast Video Downloader** into the **Applications** folder
3. Open **Finder → Applications**, right-click the app, choose **Open**

That's it.

---

## First Launch

macOS may say the app is from an unidentified developer. This happens with apps distributed outside the Mac App Store.

- Click **Cancel** on the warning
- Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**
- Click **Open**

---

## How to Use

1. Paste a video URL (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Reddit, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Twitch, and 1000+ more)
2. Click **Get Info**
3. Pick a quality — 4K, 1080p60, 1080p30, 720p, or Audio MP3
4. Choose where to save it
5. Click **Download**

To download only part of a video, enter start and end timestamps before clicking Download.

Open the gear icon (top-right) to configure browser cookie sync, download threads, and custom user-agent.
